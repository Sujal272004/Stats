Frequently asked questions

Yes. Our programmes are designed to support remote participation with structured briefs, mentor checkpoints, and flexible progress windows.

No. Each domain has a starting point for learners building their foundations. We can help you choose the right track based on your current level.

You work toward a practical project outcome and receive programme completion documentation. The project is designed to give you something useful beyond a certificate.
